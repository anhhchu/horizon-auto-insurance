West Region Renewal Notice Requirements

Document NTC-WEST-2026 · Version 1.4 · Effective 2026-01-01 · Owner: Horizon Compliance · Fictional document for the Horizon Auto Insurance demo.

The notice periods in this document are illustrative values for a fictional insurer. They are not legal requirements of any real state. Real filings must follow each state's insurance code and department guidance.

Section 1. When notice is required

1.1 Changes requiring notice. Written notice to the customer is required before renewal for a deductible change (DEDUCTIBLE_REVIEW), a coverage reduction, or a non-renewal. Safe-Driving Coaching offers and NO_ACTION do not require notice.

1.2 Human Renewal Review. Recording HUMAN_RENEWAL_REVIEW does not itself require notice. If the Senior Underwriter's outcome changes deductible or coverage, the notice periods in Section 2 apply from the date of that outcome.

2.1 Counting. Notice periods are calendar days before the renewal date, counted from the mailing date. Add 5 days when notice is sent by mail only.

2.2 Late notice. If the remaining days before renewal are fewer than the notice period, the change cannot take effect at this renewal. Record the permitted action now (usually SAFE_DRIVING_COACHING) and revisit at the next renewal, using override category OVR-05 if this differs from the recommendation.

Section 3. Required notice content

Every notice of a deductible change must include:

  1. The current and new collision deductible amounts.
  2. The effective date of the change (the renewal date).
  3. A plain-language reason based on observable driving factors, for example "frequent hard braking recorded over the last 90 days". Do not cite the claim risk probability or risk band.
  4. How to request a review of the decision, including the phone line 1-800-555-0147 and a 30-day window to respond.
  5. A statement that the customer may enroll in the Safe-Driving Program if eligible.

Section 4. Adverse action explanation

4.1 When telematics data contributes to a deductible change or non-renewal, the notice must list up to four principal driving factors in order of contribution.

4.2 Factors must come from the approved list: speeding events, hard-brake events, phone-distraction events, night-time mileage, severe-weather mileage, and prior claims.

4.3 Prohibited factors in UW-PA-2026 Section 6 must never appear in a notice.

Section 5. Record keeping

5.1 The decision, override reason, and notice date are recorded in the Horizon audit log (audit_events) and retained for 7 years.

5.2 Compliance samples 5% of deductible-change notices each quarter for content review.
