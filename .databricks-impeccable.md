## Design Context

### Surface type
Customer-facing slide deck

### Audience
Insurance business executives, underwriting leaders, data and AI platform owners, and field demo audiences

### Theme
Light

### Use case
Explain how Horizon Auto Insurance detects worsening driving risk early, guides a human underwriter to a governed renewal decision, and records every action for audit.

### Brand (locked)
- Palette: Lava 600 #FF3621 (10% accent), Navy 900 #0B2026, Light #F9F7F4
- Typography: DM Sans 400/500/700 + DM Mono
- Components: Du Bois first
- Voice: Databricks editorial-plain (no superlatives, plain statements)

### Additional constraints
The deck must remain fully editable in PowerPoint and clearly separate modeled prototype outcomes from actual customer results.
